What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Entries may include categories, seller links, listed prices, sizing notes and reference images. Treat each entry as a starting point: a spreadsheet listing does not verify quality, authenticity or current availability.

The shortlist method

Three steps from find to decision.

A useful Oopbuy finds list helps you compare like with like. Keep the category narrow and the product notes specific.

01

Choose the right category

Start an Oopbuy shoes spreadsheet search with footwear, or use an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet approach for tees, layers and coordinated sets. Separate accessories so their measurements and materials stay easy to compare.

02

Read beyond the picture

Record the exact color, variant and size. Compare garment measurements with something you already own. For shoes, check the listing’s sizing guidance; for jewelry and headwear, check dimensions and closures.

03

Compare the complete cost

Review the current item price alongside applicable service fees, shipping and taxes. An Oopbuy shopping guide should help you ask better questions before you commit, especially when a listing leaves details unclear.

Quick answers

Before you
follow a find.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. Its shopping links open CAFANS or the specified CAFANS category pages. It does not provide an Oopbuy account service or checkout.

Can I download a spreadsheet from this page?

This page provides a browser-based category directory and comparison guide, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet file. You can organize your own shortlist using the fields in the comparison table.

What should I check before choosing a product?

Review the current product listing, exact variant, size measurements, material description, seller terms and available product photos. Confirm availability and calculate applicable fees, shipping and taxes before payment.

Are the brand illustrations proof of product availability?

No. The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès illustrations are editorial category references. They are not product photographs, inventory confirmations or guarantees of authenticity. Check the destination listing for product-specific information.

Where do the shopping links go?

General discovery buttons open https://www.cafans.com. Category links open the corresponding CAFANS category, including shoes, hoodies, T-shirts, jackets, pants, headwear, sets, underwear, jerseys and accessories.
